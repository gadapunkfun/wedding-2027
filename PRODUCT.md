# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS (plus minimal vanilla JS only where needed), no build step. Hosted on GitHub Pages with custom domain `robertandgema2027.se` (CNAME). English at `/`, Spanish at `/es/`, Croatian at `/hr/`.

## Users

Wedding guests of Robert & Gema: family & friends from Croatia, Spain, Sweden and elsewhere, many opening the link on a phone from an invitation message. Mixed ages, including older relatives; some read only Spanish, Croatian or English. Their job: learn where/when each celebration is, what to wear, reply (RSVP), and optionally contribute.

## Product Purpose

One bilingual home for the couple's two 2027 weddings: one in Sweden, one in Madrid. Success = every guest knows which celebration(s) they're invited to, when & where, what to wear, and has replied via the RSVP form.

## Positioning

Two celebrations, two countries, one couple: Robert (Croatian) & Gema (fully Spanish; Spanish name: GEMA), who live together in Sweden. One wedding where they live (Sweden), one in Gema's home country (Madrid). Croatian family attends too, so Croatian cultural touches are welcome (Croatian copy at /hr/ since 2026-11-19). Neither copy is a translation afterthought.

## Operating Context

- Link shared via invitation / messaging apps; mostly mobile.
- RSVP via Google Form (couple creates it; site links/embeds it).
- Guests may attend one or both weddings.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sections: schedule / programme (per wedding), dress code, RSVP, contributions ("if you want to help with the wedding").
- No server: RSVP lives in Google Forms.
- Language switch EN <-> ES on every page; `hreflang` alternates.
- UNDECIDED: exact dates, venues, towns in Sweden, schedule times, dress code specifics, payment details for contributions (e.g. Swish / Bizum / IBAN), Google Form URL. Ship as clearly marked placeholders; never invent.

## Brand Commitments

- Names: Robert & Gema. Gema is Spanish; never anglicise or misspell (not "Gemma").
- Domain: robertandgema2027.se.
- Languages: English, Spanish & Croatian (no Swedish copy).
- Visual world pinned by the couple (2026-11-19): classy & sexy, oxblood on warm paper, editorial serif stationery (reference: Cordially "Aditya & Priya" template). Never publish reference images.

## Evidence on Hand

No photos, dates, venues or copy supplied yet. Must not fabricate venues, times, love story, or payment details.

## Product Principles

1. Both languages are first-class: equal care, idiomatic copy, one tap to switch.
2. The two weddings are distinct and clear: guests instantly see which is which.
3. Practical answers first on mobile: when, where, wear, reply.
4. Contributions are offered warmly & optionally, never pushy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Legible for older relatives: generous text size, strong contrast, no info conveyed by colour alone, works without JS.
